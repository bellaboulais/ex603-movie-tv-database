# Unit 4 Finding

## Task 4.3 - Write the Finding

The breakdown
A short breakdown for the leadership team, in /analysis/unit4-finding.md. What 
is the true failure rate, how does it vary across producers, and which two or 
three producers would you actually investigate? Report by identifier. Write for 
a reader who does not write SQL.

Reflection (150–250 words)
Conditional aggregation computes several answers in one pass over the data. 
Contrast that with running two separate filtered queries. At a million rows, why
does the difference matter?
